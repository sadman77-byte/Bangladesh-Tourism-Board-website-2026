# Visit Bangladesh – Official Foreign Tourism Portal

**Discover South Asia’s Hidden Green Gem**

A modern, AI-powered tourism website designed specifically for international travelers exploring Bangladesh. From the world’s longest natural sea beach to the mystical cloud valleys of the Chittagong Hill Tracts, this platform helps foreign tourists plan, book, and experience Bangladesh with confidence.

Built with **Google AI Studio**.

---

## Project Overview

Visit Bangladesh is a comprehensive digital gateway for foreign tourists. It combines stunning visual storytelling, practical travel tools, AI assistance, visa support, and multi-language accessibility into one seamless experience.

### Core Vision
To make Bangladesh one of the most accessible and attractive destinations in South Asia for international visitors by removing language barriers, simplifying visa processes, and providing reliable, real-time travel information.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Cinematic Hero Video Slider
- Full-screen video background slider showcasing Bangladesh’s natural beauty
- Dynamic slides featuring:
  - Cox’s Bazar sunrise & golden beach
  - Sajek Valley cloud oceans
  - Sundarbans mangrove forest & Royal Bengal Tiger
  - Sreemangal tea gardens
  - Saint Martin’s coral island
  - Bandarban hills & waterfalls
- Smooth auto-play with manual navigation and subtle overlays

### 2. AI Travel Assistant (“Sonar Bangla AI Guide”)
- Powered by advanced AI (Gemini)
- Instant answers to any travel-related question
- Custom itinerary generation
- Multi-language conversation support
- Real-time recommendations based on season, budget, interests, and travel style
- Language help and cultural tips

### 3. Comprehensive Destination Guide
- Detailed information on all major and hidden tourist destinations
- Categories: Beaches, Hills & Mountains, Wildlife, Heritage, Tea Gardens, Islands, Rivers & Wetlands
- Each destination includes:
  - High-quality photo/video gallery
  - Best time to visit
  - How to reach
  - Estimated costs
  - Recommended duration
  - Nearby attractions
  - Safety tips

### 4. Smart Booking System
- Tour packages (group & private)
- Hotel & resort booking
- Transport options (domestic flights, AC buses, trains, private cars)
- Activity & experience booking (boat safaris, trekking, cultural shows)
- Secure payment integration ready

### 5. Visa & Entry Support
- Interactive Visa Eligibility Checker (select passport country)
- Visa on Arrival information
- e-Visa guidance
- Digital Arrival Pass / Entry Form assistance
- Required documents checklist
- Official helplines and embassy contacts

### 6. Multi-Language Support
- Full website available in multiple languages
- Language switcher with support for:
  - English
  - Chinese (Simplified & Traditional)
  - Arabic
  - Hindi
  - Japanese
  - Spanish
  - French
  - German
  - Russian
  - and more
- AI Assistant also responds in the user’s preferred language

### 7. Practical Travel Tools
- Live Currency Exchange Calculator
- Bengali Audio Phrasebook with natural pronunciation
- Seasonal Climate Overview
- Tourist Police Helpline & Safety Center
- Interactive Tourism Hubs Map
- Offline-ready essential information

### 8. Modern User Experience
- Fully responsive (Mobile-first design)
- Dark elegant theme optimized for travel content
- Fast loading and smooth animations
- Accessibility-focused interface
